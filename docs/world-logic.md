# World logic (`scene/world/core/`, namespace `world`)

The ECS content of the world, built on the generic `core/` ECS. It lives under `scene/world/` but is raylib-free and view-free: it never includes `ui/`, raylib or anything else of `scene/` (not `render/`, `WorldModel`, `WorldView`); it may use `input/` actions, injected as an `input::InputHandler`. Subfolders add no namespace, and the folder name is not the namespace (`world::`; do not confuse it with the generic `core::`).

## Systems

`system.hpp`: the `System` base class (`update(dt)`; a system gets the registry and bus in its constructor). Order in `WorldModel`'s list is order of execution:

1. `PlayerInputSystem(registry, input)`: sets the `Mover` direction of `PlayerControlled` entities from the held `Move*` actions (screen space turned into world space).
2. `RouteSystem(registry, bus, terrain, network)`: rebuilds the `RouteNetwork` on its first update (the buildings of a loaded save) and on the update after any `BuildingPlaced` (once per frame however many); never otherwise. `WorkerSystem(registry, bus, factory, terrain)`: spawns `HeadquartersWorkers` (100) around the headquarters when its `BuildingPlaced` arrives (and a finished `Housing` building's workers around it), each on its own free tile found by `spawn_tile()` (`spawn.hpp`, shared with the soldiers: nearest free ring first, up to `MaxSpawnRing`; on the building only when none is free); each update an idle `Worker` takes the first construction site with room (`max_workers(site)` = effort / `EffortPerWorker` (10): a mine of 300 takes 30), becomes `Working`, sets its `Builder` site and follows the route headquarters -> site (`RouteEnd::Stop`); when the site is finished or gone it turns `Idle` and walks the route back (it can take a new site on the way). On `WorkerAssignmentRequested{building, count}` it sends `count` idle workers to a finished building with `Staffing` (capped at `needed` minus those already assigned; the job is `Worker::workplace`), a negative count sends that many back idle along the route; production jobs are never given automatically and are kept when sites appear. No headquarters = no jobs. `RouteFollowSystem(registry, network)`: steers every `RouteFollower` (see Pathfinding); once a follower stood on its Stop end it is `settled` and stays wherever it is spread within `SettleRadius` (1 tile) of it instead of walking back to the centre. `UnitTrafficSystem(registry)` (between it and `CharacterMoveSystem`): moving units within `SeparationRadius` (0.375) push each other sideways only (perpendicular to their heading; straight ahead/behind = to the right), capped at `MaxSidewaysPush` (0.5) so a unit always advances; standing units do not push walkers; instead a standing unit within `SeparationRadius` of any other unit gets a `UnitTraffic::spread` away from it (summed, up to `SpreadSpeed` 1 tile/s; units on the exact same spot split by id), which the `CharacterMoveSystem` applies (state stays Idle) only into free space: never into water or a building, and a unit inside a building (a worker at work, or the crowd waiting in the headquarters) spreads only over that building's footprint, so crowds fan out where there is room and stay packed where there is none; neighbours come from a per-tile index. It also keeps each unit's `UnitTraffic` (`components/unit.hpp`, not saved): a unit trying to walk for `StuckTime` (2 s) at under 10% of its step becomes a ghost (no pushes, no collision) until it is a tile further, so no jam lasts. `HaulSystem(registry, bus, network)` (after `CharacterMoveSystem`): on `HaulersRequested{route, mode, count}` turns idle workers into `Hauler`s of a Logistic route (other routes ignored; needs a headquarters) and sends them along headquarters -> storage, a negative count sends that many back idle; on `HaulModeRequested{route, mode}` switches the route's haulers. A hauler on the storage's tile joins the logistic route (`RouteEnd::Loop`); at each end it first unloads what fits if that end receives (storage when the mode takes, producer when it puts), then if that end sends (producer when it takes, storage when it puts) an empty hauler fills its one slot with the first item in a general slot there (filtered input slots stay) that the other end wants (a destination with filtered sections wants only those items, while they have room; one without wants anything), at the other end it unloads what fits. A hauler whose route or building is gone turns idle and walks home. The `WorkerSystem` leaves haulers alone.
3. `CharacterMoveSystem(registry, bus, terrain)`: moves entities with `Character` + `Position` + `Mover` (normalized direction, so diagonals are not faster); the `PlayerControlled` one and every `Unit` collide with water and building footprints (a unit walks into the two buildings its `RouteFollower`'s route links; a ghost unit collides with nothing) (its body is `CharacterSize` (0.375 tiles, the drawn cube) wide; a blocked step goes as far as it fits on each axis, so it stops touching the obstacle and slides along it; moves freely while already overlapping one so it can get out; other characters are not blocked), sets the state and publishes `CharacterStateChanged{entity}` only when a character starts or stops moving. A moving character with a `Facing` turns it to `facing_of(mover.direction)`; one standing still keeps its last facing.
4. `CameraFollowSystem(registry, camera)`: centres the camera on the `PlayerControlled` entity.
5. `BuildSystem`: builds on `BuildRequested`, paid by the player, and publishes `BuildingPlaced{tile}` (sites included); each placement is logged (`Placed <name> at (x, y)`). `ConstructionWorkSystem`: each `Builder` within `BuildReach` of its site's footprint edge adds `rate * dt`; at the effort the site is removed, its builders stop and `BuildingCompleted` is published.
6. `InventoryTransferSystem`: on `ItemTransferRequested{from, to, item, count}` runs `transfer_items()` when both have an `Inventory` and are within `TransferReach` (2 tiles; `within_transfer_reach()`, measured to a building's footprint edge).
7. `PowerSystem(registry)`: one global power network. Supply = `output_kw` of every finished `Generator`: `power_kw` for one without `input` (solar); one with an `input` needs `rate` stacks/s from its `Inventory` for full power, burns whole stacks into its `fuel` buffer as needed, and its `efficiency` (0..1) follows the share of the needed fuel it got, smoothed over `FuelSmoothingTime` (10 s), so `output_kw = power_kw * efficiency` (fed half its rate = about half its power; a new or reloaded one warms up from 0); demand = `demand_kw` of every finished `PowerConsumer`. When demand > supply every consumer gets `powered = false`, otherwise `true` (equal is enough). Sets `Generator::running` (output > 0). `supply_kw()` / `demand_kw()` of the last update. Generator fuel, efficiency and `powered` are not saved (recomputed next frame).
8. `ProductionSystem(registry, terrain)`: finished buildings with `Producer` + `Inventory` (and no unpowered `PowerConsumer`): progress += rate * dt, each whole unit makes one output into the building's inventory; waits with at most one unit banked while the input is missing or the output does not fit. Sets the `BuildingStatus` (when present) to `Working`, or `Idle` with the first reason: `NoPower`, `NoResource` (no output, a mine off ore), `NoWorkers`, `MissingInput`, `OutputFull`. A building with `Staffing` runs at `rate * min(present, needed) / needed`, where present = workers whose `workplace` is it and that stand within `BuildReach`; none present = `NoWorkers`. `item_from_tile()`. Production progress is not saved.

## Components

- `Facing{direction}` (`components/facing.hpp`, not saved, South by default): one of the 8 screen compass `Direction`s (North = screen up, clockwise: N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW); `spawn_character()` adds it, so the player, workers and soldiers all have one. `facing_of(world_direction)` snaps a world movement to the closest of the eight as seen on screen (world (-1, -1) = North, (1, 0) = SouthEast).
- `Character` (marks a character, holds its `CharacterState`: `Idle` or `Moving`), `Position` (a `WorldPoint`), `Mover` (speed in tiles/s + direction intent), `PlayerControlled`.
- `Inventory` (`inventory.hpp` + `.cpp`): a fixed number of `ItemStack` slots; `add()` returns what did not fit and tops up same-type stacks before using empty slots; `remove()` returns what it took, from the last slots first; `count()`, `free_slots()`. Item filter: `append(count, ItemType)` adds a filtered section (slots that accept only that item; `filter(index)`, empty = general slot); an item with filtered slots in the inventory only goes there, never into general slots; `set_slot()` ignores a stack its slot refuses. Filters come from the blueprint and are not saved. `transfer_items(from, to, item, count)` moves as much as the source holds and the target fits and returns how many moved.
- `building.hpp`: `TileSize { width, height }` (default 1x1), `Building { type, tile, size }` (`tile` = origin, the corner with the smallest x and y; covers `size` tiles towards +x/+y), `ConstructionSite { progress, effort }` (present while unfinished), `Producer { recipe, progress }` (its own copy of the recipe so the rate can change per building), `BuildingStatus { state, reason }` with `BuildingState` (`Working`, `Idle`) and `IdleReason` (`None`, `NoPower`, `NoResource`, `MissingInput`, `OutputFull`); set by the building's systems every frame, not saved.
- `power.hpp`: `Generator { spec, fuel, efficiency, output_kw, running }`, `PowerConsumer { demand_kw, powered }` (only whether power arrives; whether the building works is its `BuildingStatus`).
- `power_report.hpp`: `power_report(registry)` -> `PowerReport { generators, consumers, produced_kw, demand_kw, overloaded() }`, each a `PowerEntry { id, name (entity label), kw, active }`; finished buildings only, as the PowerSystem left them (a generator's `kw` is its current `output_kw`).
- `builder.hpp`: `Builder { rate, site }` (`CharacterBuildRate` 10/s, `BuildReach` 2 tiles; every character has one).
- `worker.hpp`: `Worker { state, workplace }`, `Staffing { needed }` (workers for the full production rate) with `WorkerState` (`Idle`, `Working`), `EffortPerWorker`, `HeadquartersWorkers`, `max_workers()`. The job is the `Builder`'s site. Saved by `WorkerCodec` (state, workplace, site and walked route, by building tile); a loaded worker carries a `PendingWorkerLinks` until the `WorkerSystem`'s next update turns the tiles into ids (a building no longer there = idle).
- `hauler.hpp`: `Hauler { route, mode, joined }`, `HaulMode` (`Take`, `Put`, `Both`; `takes()` / `puts()`, `haul_mode(take, put)`; saved as its `to_string()`). Saved by `HaulerCodec`; a loaded hauler carries a `PendingHaulerLinks { from, to, mode, joined, forward }` (tiles) until the `HaulSystem`'s next update restores the `Hauler` and its follower (the loop if it had joined, else headquarters -> storage); a missing building or route = idle. The `WorkerSystem` keeps such a worker `Working`.
- `logistics_report.hpp`: `logistics_report(registry, network)` -> one `LogisticEntry { key, from, to, length, haulers, mode, to_type }` per Logistic route (`mode` = its first hauler's), grouped by destination type in `BuildingType` order (stable: network order within a group).
- `worker_report.hpp`: `worker_report(registry)` -> `WorkerReport { idle, workplaces }`, each a `WorkplaceEntry { id, name, tile, assigned, needed }`; finished buildings with `Staffing` only, in registry order. `is_at_work(registry, worker)`: `Working` and within `BuildReach` of its workplace (or, without one, its `Builder` site); the ProductionSystem counts these as present and the `EntityRenderer` does not draw them (they are inside the building).
- `persistent.hpp`: `Persistent{archetype}`; only entities with it are saved.

## Pathfinding

`pathfinding/` computes walks between buildings once, not per frame:

- `pathfinder.hpp`: `find_path(start, goal, is_blocked, max_nodes = MaxPathSearchNodes)`, A* over 8 neighbours (diagonal cost sqrt(2), no corner cutting: a diagonal needs both orthogonal neighbours free). `start` and `goal` are always walkable (they are buildings). The world has no edge, so the search gives up (none) after `max_nodes` (20000) expanded tiles.
- `route_network.hpp`: `RouteKey{from, to}` (building ids, stable across rebuilds), `Route{key, tiles, kind}` (from the `from` tile to the `to` tile, both included; empty = unreachable; `RouteKind::Worker` or `Logistic`). `RoutePlanner::plan()` returns `RouteRequest{key, kind}`s: `HeadquartersRoutePlanner` = Worker routes from the headquarters to every other building; `LogisticRoutePlanner` = Logistic routes from every Storage to every building with a `Producer` or a fuelled `Generator` (one with an `input`); `CompositeRoutePlanner` concatenates planners; `default_route_planner()` = HQ + logistic (used by `WorldModel`). A new combination = a new planner subclass. `RouteNetwork(planner)`: `rebuild(registry, terrain)` (blocked = water or a tile of any building other than the route's two ends, sites included; a route starts on the `from` origin and may cross its own buildings' footprints), `find(key)`, `revision()` (grows each rebuild); each rebuild is logged with its revision, route count and unreachable count. Owned by `WorldModel` (`routes()`); not saved, recomputed after loading.
- `components/route_follower.hpp`: `RouteFollower{route, end, waypoint, forward, revision}` signs an entity with `Position` + `Mover` up to a route; `RouteEnd::Stop` stands still on the last tile, `RouteEnd::Loop` walks back and forth. `RouteFollowSystem` points the `Mover` at the next waypoint, `LaneOffset` (0.2) right of its centre along the walking direction, so the two directions of a route walk on two lanes and pass each other; it moves on within `ArriveRadius` (0.3) or once past the waypoint, so a pushed follower never circles it; a `Stop` end is reached exactly on the last tile's centre; after a rebuild it rejoins at the nearest waypoint (ties go forward); no route or unreachable = stands still. Not for `PlayerControlled` entities. Not saved.

## Occupancy

`occupancy.hpp`: `footprint_tiles(building)`, `footprint_centre(origin, size)`, `Occupancy` (tile -> the buildings on it, latest wins, so one passing over a tile, like a loaded building created on (0, 0) before it is moved, never hides the one standing there) and `building_at(registry, tile)` (0 = none), the one way to ask which building covers a tile. `track_buildings(registry)` puts an `Occupancy` in the registry's context, indexes the buildings already there and keeps it current through the `Building` add/remove hooks (loaded saves included); `spawn_building()`, `make_building()` and `WorldModel` call it, and it is idempotent. On an untracked registry `building_at` scans the buildings.

## Items

`items/item.hpp`: `ItemType` (`IronOre`, `CopperOre`, `Coal`, `Steel`, `Electronics`, `Tool`, `Ammo`, `Weapon`), `Item { type, max_stack }` (definitions in `items::`, 100 each except Weapon: 1 per slot), `ItemStack { item, count }`, `to_string(ItemType)`.

## Entities

- `entities/character.hpp`: `spawn_character()` (`"character"` archetype): `Character` + `Position` + `Mover` at `CharacterSpeed` + an `Inventory` of `CharacterInventorySlots` (40) + a `Builder`. A new game's player gets `StartingIronOre` (200).
- `entities/unit.hpp`: `spawn_unit()`: a character + the `Unit` marker (`components/unit.hpp`), the base archetype of every simulation-driven character (never the player). Units "inherit" by composition: a unit archetype is `spawn_unit()` plus its own component.
- `worker_type.hpp`: `WorkerType { None, Builder, Miner, Hauler, Steelworker, Technician, Toolmaker, Munitioner, Gunsmith }` and `worker_type(registry, worker)`: derived from the current job, never stored (a `Hauler` = Hauler; a workplace = its building's type, a Mine = Miner, a Steelworks = Steelworker, an Electronic Factory = Technician, a Tool Factory = Toolmaker, an Ammo Factory = Munitioner, a Weapon Factory = Gunsmith; a `Builder` site under construction = Builder; idle = None, shown as "Idle"). A new production building = a new case in `type_at()`.
- `entities/worker.hpp`: `spawn_worker()` (`"worker"` archetype, `WorkerArchetype`): a unit + `Worker`, with an `Inventory` of `WorkerInventorySlots` (1) instead of the character's 40. Drawn as a cube like the player, labelled with its worker type ("Worker (Miner)", "Worker (Idle)").
- `entities/soldier.hpp`: `spawn_soldier()` (`"soldier"` archetype, `SoldierArchetype`): a unit + `Soldier` (`components/military.hpp`), with an `Inventory` of `SoldierInventorySlots` (2). Saved through its archetype and the `InventoryCodec`.
- `systems/recruit_system.hpp`: `RecruitSystem(registry, bus, factory, terrain)` spawns one soldier on the centre of the nearest free tile around the building (ring by ring up to `MaxSpawnRing` (8); not a building, water or a unit's tile; on the building itself when none is free) for each `SoldierRecruitRequested { building }` (`events/military_events.hpp`, sent by `WorldModel::request_recruit()` from the building panel's Recruit button) when it has a `Recruiter`, no `ConstructionSite` and power.
- Combat: `weapon_stats(ItemType)` (`items/item.hpp`) gives a weapon item's `WeaponStats{range, damage, cooldown, mode, accuracy}` (`FireMode::SemiAutomatic` / `Automatic`; Weapon: 6 tiles, 25 hp, 1 s, semi-automatic, 0.6 accuracy; empty for other items); `held_weapon(inventory)` (`shoot_system.hpp`) = the first weapon held. `components/combat.hpp`: `Health{current}` (`MaxHealth` 100) is added by `spawn_character()` (so the player, workers and soldiers can be shot) and `Shooter{cooldown, experience}` only by `spawn_soldier()` and by the `WorldModel` to the player (workers never shoot); neither is saved. `systems/shoot_system.hpp`: `ShootSystem(registry, bus)` on `ShotRequested{shooter, target}` (`events/combat_events.hpp`) fires when the shooter holds a weapon and Ammo, is off cooldown and the target is within range (straight-line distance): spends 1 Ammo, restarts the cooldown, adds 1 experience, rolls the injected `HitRoll` (uniform [0, 1); `random_hit_roll()` by default, the `WorldModel` takes one too so tests can pass an always-hit roll) against `hit_chance(weapon, experience)` = clamp(accuracy + experience × `AccuracyPerExperience` (0.005), 0, 1), publishes `ShotFired{shooter, target, hit}` and, on a hit only, takes the damage from the target's `Health` and destroys it at 0 (a `TrainingTarget` is restored to full instead). `UnitsAttackRequested{units, target}` gives each Shooter an `AttackOrder{target, approach_goal}` (not saved): `update()` fires it whenever possible; while the target is beyond the weapon's range it publishes `UnitsApproachRequested{units, goal}` (`events/military_events.hpp`) to the target's tile, only when that tile differs from the last one asked (`AttackOrder::approach_goal`, so an approach that cannot bring it in range is not re-sent every tick), and once in range it removes the `PathFollower` to stop and fire; it drops the order when the target is gone or the ammo runs out; `UnitsMoveRequested` drops it too. `update()` also counts cooldowns down. `targeting.hpp`: `shootable_at(registry, tile, exclude)` = the building on the tile, else the first character on it, that has `Health` and is not `exclude`. The player shoots through `WorldModel::shoot_at(tile)` (see world-scene.md); soldiers do not shoot on their own yet.
- Unit commands: `selection.hpp`: `soldiers_in(registry, projection, a, b)` = the soldiers whose projected position is inside the screen box (corners in any order); `formation_tiles(goal, count, is_blocked)` = `count` distinct free tiles nearest the goal, ring by ring, within `MaxFormationRadius` (8). `components/selected.hpp`: `Selected` marker (not saved), set by `WorldModel::select_units()`. `systems/move_order_system.hpp`: `MoveOrderSystem` on `UnitsMoveRequested{units, goal}` (and on `UnitsApproachRequested`, which paths the same way but cancels no order) (sent by `WorldModel::order_move()` next tick) gives each soldier a `PathFollower` (`components/path_follower.hpp`, not saved) with `find_path()` from its tile to its formation tile (water and buildings blocked); no path = it stops. `systems/path_follow_system.hpp`: `PathFollowSystem` (after `RouteFollowSystem`) steers the `Mover` to each tile centre, stands exactly on the last one and removes the `PathFollower`. `systems/resupply_system.hpp`: `ResupplySystem` on `UnitsResupplyRequested{units, supply}` (sent by `WorldModel::order_units()` on a `Supply` building, `components/military.hpp`) gives each soldier a `ResupplyOrder` (not saved) and, unless already within `SupplyReach` (1 tile of the footprint), a path to the nearest reachable free tile beside the building; `update()` then, once it has no `PathFollower`, takes one Weapon (if it holds none) and Ammo up to one full stack from a finished supply within reach, and drops the order (also dropped on `UnitsMoveRequested`). Every `AutoResupplyInterval` (5 s) `update()` also sends each soldier with no Weapon or no Ammo, without a `PathFollower` or `ResupplyOrder`, the same way to the nearest finished `Supply` that holds something it lacks.
- `entities/building.hpp`: `spawn_building()` (`"building"` archetype) and `make_building()`: `Building` (with the construction's `size`) + `Position` at the footprint centre. Reach checks (transfer, building, workers at work) use `distance_to_building()` (`occupancy.hpp`): distance to the nearest footprint edge, 0 inside, or to the `Position` for a non-building + `blueprint.apply()` + a site when effort > 0. It knows no building feature: those come from the blueprint.
- `entities/entity_factory.hpp`: `EntityFactory` (archetype name → spawn function, injected; `make_entity_factory()` registers them; `spawn()` adds `Persistent`). `WorldModel` spawns the new-game player through it too.

- `construction/building_type.hpp`: `BuildingType` (`Headquarters`, `Mine`, `Storage`, `Steelworks`, `ElectronicFactory`, `House`, `CoalGenerator`, `ToolFactory`, `AmmoFactory`, `WeaponFactory`, `Barracks`, `TrainingTarget`; one value per implemented building; `building_type_key()` / `parse_building_type()` for saves).
- `construction/blueprint.hpp`: `Blueprint`, the prefab of a building: `with(component)` (any component type, chainable), `apply(registry, id)` (adds a copy of each; one the entity already has is kept, so a loaded inventory survives), `find<C>()` (the blueprint's data of C or null). A new building feature = a component + a `.with()` in the catalog; `Construction` and `make_building()` do not change.
- `construction/build_rule.hpp`: `BuildResult`, `has_headquarters()`, the `BuildRule` interface (`check_unlocked(registry, type)` before a tile is chosen, `check_site(terrain, tile)` once the tile is free; both default Ok) and the rules `UniqueRule` (AlreadyExists), `NeedsHeadquartersRule` (NeedsHeadquarters), `NeedsOreRule` (NeedsOre). A new placement condition = a new subclass.
- `construction/construction.hpp`: `ConstructionCategory` (`Special`, `Mine`, `Factory`, `Military`); `Construction { name, icon, type, cost, effort, rules, blueprint, size }` (`size` = its footprint, 1x1 by default; the Headquarters is 5x5 and the Mine 2x2) where `type` empty = listed but not buildable yet, `cost` = item stacks paid from the player's inventory, `effort` = work units, 0 = finished at once, `rules` = its `BuildRule`s in order, `blueprint` = its components; `ConstructionGroup`; `construction_catalog()` (by category, in build bar order) and `find_construction(type)`.   - Headquarters: 5x5, free, effort 0; rules: Unique (the only entry without NeedsHeadquarters); blueprint: solar `Generator`, 1000 kW.
  - Mine: 2x2, 100 iron ore, effort 300; rules: NeedsHeadquarters, NeedsOre; blueprint: `Inventory` 10 slots, `Producer` (no input, the ore below, 4/s at 10 workers), `PowerConsumer` 100 kW, `BuildingStatus`, `Staffing` 10.

  - Coal Generator: 3x3, 200 steel + 100 copper ore, effort 1200; rules: NeedsHeadquarters; blueprint: `Inventory` of 5 coal filtered slots only, coal `Generator` (1 coal/s for 1000 kW, output scaled by efficiency). No workers.
  - Steelworks (Factory): 3x3, 300 iron ore, effort 1200; rules: NeedsHeadquarters; blueprint: `Inventory` 10 general slots + 5 iron ore + 5 coal filtered slots, `Producer` (2 iron ore + 2 coal -> 1 steel, 2/s at 50 workers), `PowerConsumer` 200 kW, `BuildingStatus`, `Staffing` 50.
  - Electronic Factory (Factory): 3x3, 200 steel, effort 1200; rules: NeedsHeadquarters; blueprint: `Inventory` 10 general slots + 5 copper ore + 5 iron ore filtered slots, `Producer` (2 copper ore + 2 iron ore -> 1 electronics, 2/s at 50 workers), `PowerConsumer` 200 kW, `BuildingStatus`, `Staffing` 50.
  - Tool Factory (Factory): 3x3, 200 steel + 100 electronics, effort 1200; rules: NeedsHeadquarters; blueprint: `Inventory` 10 general + 5 electronics + 5 steel filtered slots, `Producer` (1 electronics + 1 steel -> 1 tool, 2/s at 50 workers), `PowerConsumer` 300 kW, `BuildingStatus`, `Staffing` 50.

- `construction/build_rules.hpp`: `check_unlocked()` (NotBuildable, then each rule's `check_unlocked()`), `can_build()` (+ TileBlocked: water or another building under any footprint tile, each rule's `check_site()`, NotEnoughResources), `build()` (applies them, pays the cost, spawns through the factory).

## Terrain

`terrain/terrain.hpp`: `Terrain(seed)`, a flat unbounded world of `TileType::Grass` / `Water` / `IronOre` / `CopperOre` / `Coal` (`is_ore()`). `tile_at(tile)` is a pure function of the seed (value noise), so a loaded save recreates the same world:

- the `SpawnClearRadius` around (0,0) is always grass;
- three medium starting ore nodes, one per type, 24–36 tiles out, each on the first dry spot around its seed-random angle (when none is fully dry, the driest one is used and its footprint turned into land), so ore never touches water;
- scattered nodes, one per 64-tile cell, only on land, none inside `StartingAreaRadius` (64), radius growing with the distance from (0,0) up to a cap; irregular edges via the noise.

## Saving entities

- `save/component_codec.hpp`: `ComponentCodec` (`write` / `read` of one component's `Fields`).
- `save/codecs.hpp`: `PositionCodec` (skips buildings: their position is always the footprint centre), `CharacterCodec` (writes and restores `Moving`/`Running` as `Idle` through `saved_state()`), `PlayerControlledCodec`, `InventoryCodec` (`inventory.<slot>=<ItemType>:<count>` per filled slot, invalid entries skipped), `BuildingCodec` (`building.type`, `building.x`, `building.y`; re-places the building), `ConstructionSiteCodec` (`construction.progress`; missing = finished), `WorkerCodec` (`worker.state`, `worker.workplace.x/y`, `worker.site.x/y`, `worker.route.x/y` + `worker.route.forward`: buildings referenced by tile since ids are not saved, resolved after loading through `PendingWorkerLinks`; a hauler's walked route is left to the next codec), `HaulerCodec` (`hauler.from.x/y`, `hauler.to.x/y`, `hauler.mode`, `hauler.joined`, `hauler.forward`, resolved through `PendingHaulerLinks`). A worker's 1-slot cargo is its `Inventory`.
- `save/world_serializer.hpp`: `WorldSerializer` (`snapshot()` / `restore()`); `make_world_serializer(factory)` lists the codecs: a new saved component = one codec there. `BuildingCodec` comes first because re-placing a building recreates its components. Entity ids are not saved. This is the one place where `world::` depends on `game/save_game.hpp` (plain data).

## Camera and isometric math

- `camera.hpp`: `Camera` (`center`, `projection(w, h)` puts the centre at the middle of the screen, `MinZoom`/`MaxZoom`). `TerrainRenderer` and `EntityRenderer` draw through its projection.
- `isometric.hpp` (2:1 diamond projection): `TileCoord`, `WorldPoint` (continuous, in tiles), `ScreenPoint`, `IsoProjection` (tile size + `origin`, the screen position of world (0,0): move it to scroll, scale the tile size to zoom) with `world_to_screen()`, `screen_to_world()`, `tile_at()` (mouse picking), `tile_center()`, `tile_corners()`; `tile_containing()` floors a world position to its tile; `depth_of()` / `draws_before()` give the back-to-front order; `painter_order(boxes)` orders `DepthBox`es (ground footprints, a point for a character) back to front: a box past another along +x or +y is drawn after it, the rest by centre depth; `screen_direction_to_world()` (screen up = world (-1,-1)).
