# Gameplay (current state)

- **Main menu**: New Game, Load Game, Settings, Quit.
- **Settings**: resolution dropdown and fullscreen checkbox, applied to the window by `Game` and saved to `settings.cfg` in the data directory, restored on the next run.
- **New Game** asks for a save name, writes `saves/<name>.sav` (overwriting a save with the same name) and enters the world scene.
- **Load Game** lists the saves, newest first (the list does not scroll), and enters the world with the chosen one.

## World

- **Pause (Esc)**: shows a menu with the save's name and creation date, a Save button (writes the save again, keeping the previous file as `<name>.sav_old`) and a Main Menu button.
- **Units** (workers): collide with water and buildings like the player, except the buildings their route links (they go into those to work, build or unload); they keep to the right of a route so opposite traffic passes, nudge each other sideways instead of blocking, and one stuck for 2 s walks through until it is a tile further.
- **Player**: drawn as a cube labelled with its name and state (`Idle` / `Moving`); walks with WASD (diagonals are not faster, stops on release); cannot walk over water or buildings and slides along them; the camera follows it so it stays at the screen centre. The mouse wheel zooms (except while a building's inventory panel is open: there it scrolls a big inventory, like a storage's, four rows at a time; and while the player menu is open, where it scrolls the Logistics page). A `CharacterStateChanged` event is published when it starts or stops moving.
- **Inventory**: 40 slots of item stacks. A new game's player starts with 200 iron ore. Tab opens the player menu (the world keeps running; Esc, Tab or its X closes it) with tabs Inventory (slot grid), Energy (power produced and consumed, overload warning, the list of generators and of consumers with their kW and whether they run) , Statistics (disabled) and Logistics (every logistic route, grouped under a heading button per destination building type such as "[-] Mine (2)"; clicking it hides that group's routes ("[+] Mine (2)") and clicking again shows them; a list taller than the page scrolls with the mouse wheel, a line per notch, with a scrollbar on the right like a storage's inventory: storage -> production building, its length in tiles and its haulers, with Take and Put checkboxes (both can be checked; one always stays checked) and -/+ to assign idle workers as haulers). A hauler walks from the headquarters to the storage, then loops along the route forever carrying one slot (a worker's inventory has 1 slot, up to a full stack) per trip: Take carries from the production building's general slots (not its filtered input slots) into the storage, Put from the storage into the production building, carrying only what it uses as inputs (an input whose slots are full stays in the storage; a building without input slots takes anything). With both checked, each trip brings the inputs (e.g. iron ore and coal to a Steelworks) and carries the output (steel) back to the storage. Haulers are saved with their route, mode, cargo and walking direction (one whose building is gone loads idle).
- **Build bar (B)**: icon tabs Special / Mines / Factories / Military, each with a drop-up list. Only these can be picked:
  - Headquarters: 5x5 tiles, free, built at once, only one.
  - Mine: 2x2 tiles, 100 iron ore, effort 300, its origin tile on ore, only once a Headquarters exists.
  - Storage: 2x2 tiles, 200 iron ore, effort 600, only once a Headquarters exists; 120-slot inventory, consumes 20 kW. Each storage gets a logistic route to every production building.
  - House (Special tab): 2x2 tiles, 100 steel, effort 300, only once a Headquarters exists; when its construction finishes it brings 50 new idle workers, standing on it; consumes 20 kW.
  - Coal Generator (Special tab): 3x3 tiles, 200 steel + 100 copper ore, effort 1200, only once a Headquarters exists; holds up to 500 coal (coal only) and burns 1 coal/s for 1000 kW. Its output follows the coal it actually gets, smoothed over about 10 s: fed half that rate it gives about 500 kW, without coal nothing. Its details show the current output and efficiency. Storages get a logistic route to it, so Put haulers can keep it fed. Needs no workers.
  - Steelworks (Factories tab): 3x3 tiles, 300 iron ore, effort 1200, only once a Headquarters exists; turns 2 iron ore + 2 coal into 1 steel, 2 steel/s at its full crew of 50 workers (Steelworkers), inventory of 10 general slots (the steel) plus 5 slots only for iron ore and 5 only for coal (empty ones show their item faintly; iron and coal never go into the general slots, and Take haulers only carry what is in the general slots), consumes 200 kW. Idle (missing input) while either input is short.
  - Electronic Factory (Factories tab): 3x3 tiles, 200 steel, effort 1200, only once a Headquarters exists; turns 2 copper ore + 2 iron ore into 1 electronics, 2 electronics/s at its full crew of 50 workers (Technicians), inventory of 10 general slots (the electronics) plus 5 slots only for copper ore and 5 only for iron ore, consumes 200 kW. Idle (missing input) while either input is short.
  - Tool Factory (Factories tab): 3x3 tiles, 200 steel + 100 electronics, effort 1200, only once a Headquarters exists; turns 1 electronics + 1 steel into 1 tool, 2 tools/s at its full crew of 50 workers (Toolmakers), 10 general slots (the tools) plus 5 slots only for electronics and 5 only for steel, consumes 300 kW.
  - Ammo Factory (Factories tab): 3x3 tiles, 200 steel + 100 tools, effort 1200, only once a Headquarters exists; turns 1 coal + 1 iron ore + 1 copper ore into 2 ammo, 2 crafts/s (4 ammo/s) at its full crew of 50 workers (Munitioners), 10 general slots (the ammo) plus 5 slots each only for coal, iron ore and copper ore, consumes 300 kW.
  - Weapon Factory (Factories tab): 3x3 tiles, 200 steel + 100 tools, effort 1200, only once a Headquarters exists; turns 1 steel + 1 tool into 1 weapon, 2 weapons/s at its full crew of 50 workers (Gunsmiths), 10 general slots (the weapons, one per slot) plus 5 slots only for steel and 5 only for tools, consumes 300 kW.
  - Barracks (Military tab): 3x3 tiles, 200 steel + 100 tools, effort 1200, only once a Headquarters exists; it is a supply: 5 slots that only take Weapons (one per slot, so 5 weapons) and 5 that only take Ammo (no general slots), consumes 200 kW, needs no workers. Its panel (click it within reach) has a **Recruit** button: each click recruits one soldier, which appears on the nearest free tile around the barracks (not a building, water or a tile another unit stands on), while it is finished and powered (free for now). A soldier is a unit with a 2-slot inventory.
- Shooting: only soldiers and the player character can shoot; workers can be shot but never shoot. They shoot when they carry a Weapon and Ammo (a new player starts with 1 Weapon and a full stack of 100 Ammo). Pressing the **left** button makes the player shoot what is on that tile (a soldier, worker or a Training Target); the Weapon is semi-automatic, so each shot needs a new click (an automatic weapon would keep firing while the button is held). Out of range, without ammo or on an empty tile nothing happens. A fired shot shows as a short yellow line from the shooter to the target. A shot spends 1 Ammo, reaches up to the weapon's range (6 tiles), deals 25 of the target's 100 health and needs 1 s before the next; a target at 0 health is removed.
  - Training Target (Military tab): 1x1 tile, free and finished as soon as it is placed, only once a Headquarters exists. Something to shoot at for practice: shots damage it, but it is never destroyed (back to full health at 0).
- Commanding soldiers: hold the **left** mouse button and drag a box to select the soldiers inside it (outlined; a box over nothing clears the selection; a plain left click clears the selection, and still builds or opens a transfer). A **right** click on something that can be shot (another soldier, a worker, a Training Target) makes every selected soldier carrying a Weapon and Ammo keep firing at it (whenever in range and ready) until it is destroyed, they run out of ammo or get a move order; soldiers do not chase it. A **right** click on a Barracks sends the selected soldiers next to it (nearest free tile beside it), where each takes one Weapon (only if it carries none) and tops its Ammo up to one full stack (100), as far as the barracks holds them (it must be finished; power is not needed); a move order on the way cancels it. Soldiers also do this on their own: every 5 s, each soldier with no Weapon or no Ammo that is standing idle walks to the nearest finished Barracks that holds something it lacks. Otherwise a right click sends the selection to the tile under the pointer: each soldier gets its own tile around it (the nearest free ones, skipping water and buildings) and walks an A* path there, around buildings and water.
- **Workers**: placing the Headquarters brings 100 workers (drawn as cubes, labelled Idle / Working). Idle workers go by themselves to any construction site, walking the precomputed route from the Headquarters, and build it like the player does; a site takes at most effort / 10 workers (30 on a mine). When the site is finished they turn idle and walk back to the Headquarters.
- **Production workers**: a Mine needs 10 workers at it for its full rate (4 ore/s); with fewer it produces proportionally slower (5 workers = half), with none it is idle (no workers). Workers are never sent to production by themselves: in the player menu (Tab), the **Workers** tab shows the idle workers and every finished production building with its assigned/needed workers; `+` sends one idle worker there (along the route from the Headquarters), `-` sends one back. A worker doing its job (at its mine or construction site) is inside the building and disappears from the view; it shows up again when it walks away. Workers are saved with their position, state, job and the route they are walking, so a loaded game continues where it was.
  With a selection, a translucent box covering its whole footprint previews it from the tile under the mouse (green = buildable, red = not); clicking places it; a refused click keeps the selection and shows a stronger red. Buildings are one-tile-tall boxes over their footprint (Headquarters 5x5x1, Mine 2x2x1): blue when finished, grey with a % while a construction site.
- **Building**: holding the left button over a site within 2 tiles of its edge builds it at 10 effort/s (a mine takes 30 s).
- **Mines** produce the ore under them (iron, copper or coal) at 4/s into a 10-slot inventory (saved; stops while full). Each finished mine consumes 100 kW.
- **Power**: the Headquarters generates 1000 kW of solar power (no input). When the total demand of finished consumers exceeds the supply, all of them lose power and their buildings go idle (reason: no power) and stop producing until the supply covers the demand again (10 mines run, the 11th idles them all).
- **Building panel**: clicking a finished mine within 2 tiles of its edge opens it: the player's inventory above the mine's name, state (with the idle reason), power consumption and inventory; clicking a filled slot moves its stack across. X, Esc or walking away closes it.
- **Terrain**: flat, seed-generated: grass (green), water (blue), iron (grey), copper (orange), coal (black). Three medium ore nodes near the spawn, bigger ones further out. Recreated from the seed on load.
- All icons are the placeholder (fallback) texture. There is no other gameplay yet.

## What a save holds

Name, creation time, world seed and the persistent entities: the Player (position restored; state always saved as `Idle`; inventory) and buildings (type, tile, construction progress, inventory).
